# Privacy / legal launch checklist

This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice.

- Identify the legal entity/controller responsible for the meeting website.
- Complete the Imprint with the details actually required for that entity.
- Complete the privacy notice under GDPR Article 13 for website access, abstract submission and registration.
- List all processors/subprocessors (hosting, form backend/database, email delivery).
- Execute data-processing agreements where required.
- Use HTTPS end-to-end.
- Keep API/database/mail credentials server-side only.
- Validate and rate-limit form submissions server-side; add spam protection that does not introduce unnecessary tracking if possible.
- Define access permissions for organisers.
- Define retention periods and deletion procedures.
- Treat optional dietary/accessibility information as potentially sensitive; collect only what is necessary and keep it optional.
- Reassess consent/cookie requirements before launch if analytics, embedded maps/videos, social-media widgets, externally hosted fonts or other client-side storage/access are added.
- Verify the final wording with the responsible institution / data-protection contact before publication.
