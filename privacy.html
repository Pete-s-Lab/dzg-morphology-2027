Data protection

Privacy information

Prototype only — this must be completed once the controller, hosting and form-backend providers are fixed.

Draft: Do not publish this page unchanged. The actual services, legal bases, retention periods and recipient information must match the final deployment.

1. Controller

Name and contact details of the responsible organisation

2. Website hosting

The final notice should identify the hosting provider, the technical log data processed when the site is accessed, the purpose/legal basis, recipients and retention period.

3. Abstract submission

The form will process author names, affiliations, submitter email, title, abstract, presentation preference, career status, keywords and optional comments for reviewing and organising scientific contributions. The final notice must state the legal basis, recipients, retention period and any processors used for storage or email delivery.

4. Registration

The registration form will process name, affiliation, email and organisational choices. Optional dietary or accessibility information may contain sensitive personal data and therefore needs particularly careful handling and an appropriate legal basis.

5. Cookies and local storage

The prototype does not set analytics, advertising or preference cookies and does not use browser local storage. If this remains true in production, a consent banner should not be necessary solely for this website. If analytics, embedded media or other non-essential storage/access are added, this section and the consent mechanism must be revisited.

6. Data retention

Define specific retention periods for registrations, accepted/rejected abstracts and optional sensitive information.

7. Data-subject rights

The final notice should explain the applicable GDPR rights, how to exercise them, and the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.
